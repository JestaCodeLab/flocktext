# Why FlockText Costs Half What Sending SMS Directly Through Your Telco Does

If you've ever sent a promotion or a reminder to customers one text at a time from your own phone, you already know the real cost isn't just the money — it's the time. But the money adds up faster than most people realize, too.

Here's the actual math.

## What sending directly through your telco costs

Sending SMS the "default" way — one message at a time from a personal line, or through a telco's own retail SMS pricing — means paying **GHS 0.060 per SMS**.

Send that to a list of 1,000 customers, and you're looking at:

**1,000 × GHS 0.060 = GHS 60.00**

And that's before accounting for the time spent copying numbers, pasting the same message repeatedly, and hoping nothing gets sent twice — or missed.

## What it costs on FlockText

FlockText's pay-as-you-go rate is **GHS 0.0280 per SMS credit** — no monthly contract, no minimum spend, credits used on your own schedule.

The same 1,000-message send:

**1,000 × GHS 0.0280 = GHS 28.00**

That's less than **half** what the same send costs going directly through a telco — a savings of GHS 32.00, or **53%**, for the identical message, reaching the identical customers.

## Why the gap exists

It's not a discount trick — it comes down to how the two pricing models work:

- **Telco retail SMS pricing** is built for individual, person-to-person texting: one plan, one line, priced for occasional personal use, not for sending the same message to a thousand people at once.
- **A dedicated bulk SMS platform** buys sending capacity at wholesale/API rates and passes the lower cost through to you, while adding the tools retail texting doesn't have — scheduling, contact groups, delivery reports, and templates — at no extra charge.

You're not paying more for fewer features. You're paying less, for more.
